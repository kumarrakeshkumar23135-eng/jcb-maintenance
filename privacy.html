Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information JCB Maintenance Services may receive when you visit this website or send a service request, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you. Please read it before submitting personal information.

1. Information we may collect

When you use the service-request form, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name and mobile number
  • Email address, if you provide one
  • Worksite or machine location
  • JCB model, machine details and problem description
  • Photos or other files you choose to upload
  • Any other details you voluntarily include in your message

The website's hosting provider or other technical services may also process basic technical information, such as browser or device details, security logs and information needed to deliver the website. The exact information depends on the services in use.

2. How we use information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Review and respond to your service request
  • Contact you about the reported machine issue or service arrangements
  • Understand the issue using the description, location and photos you provide
  • Maintain service records, handle enquiries and improve the website
  • Meet legal obligations and protect the website and our rights

3. Service requests, forms and uploaded photos

Information and files submitted through the service-request form may be processed by the website's form and hosting provider (for example, Netlify if Netlify Forms is enabled for this site) and made available to the site operator so the request can be reviewed. Their handling of information is also subject to the provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Please upload only photos relevant to the machine problem. Avoid including identity documents, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information in photos or descriptions.

4. Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

This website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your browser, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted by your settings and applicable rules, show personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services in Google's Privacy Policy. You can also review or change available ad-personalization settings in Google Ads Settings. Blocking cookies in your browser may affect some website or advertising features.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that help operate the website, process forms, host content, provide security or deliver advertisements. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, the website or our legal rights. Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the way this website is operated to protect information. No method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be promised. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the service request, business records, legal requirements or handling disputes, and is then deleted or otherwise dealt with where practicable.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit optional information, although this may limit our ability to respond to a service request. You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or to request correction or deletion. We may need to retain some information where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping, and we may need to verify your request.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for people seeking construction-equipment maintenance information or services, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, advertising arrangements or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new update date. Please review this page periodically.
