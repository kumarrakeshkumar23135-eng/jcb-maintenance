About JCB Maintenance Services

Clear information and a simple way to request machine maintenance support.

JCB Maintenance Services is a service-request website for machine owners, operators and contractors who need to report equipment problems or ask about routine maintenance. Customers can share their contact details, worksite location, machine information and a description of the issue. A photo can also be attached to help explain the reported problem.

Service requests are reviewed by the manager, Munshi Ram, who can contact the customer to discuss the request and service availability. On-site support depends on the location, type of work and availability. Submitting a request does not by itself confirm a booking or a guaranteed response time.

JCB Maintenance Guides

General information to help operators plan routine checks and communicate equipment concerns.

A daily walk-around check

Before operating equipment, follow the checks in the machine's operator manual. Look for visible leaks, damaged parts, loose components, tyre or track concerns and warning indicators. Report anything unusual and do not operate equipment if you believe it may be unsafe.

Keep maintenance records

Record service dates, machine hours, work completed and concerns noticed by the operator. Use the maintenance schedule specified by the manufacturer for the exact model. Clear records help the service team understand the machine's history.

Describe a machine problem clearly

When requesting support, mention the machine model, worksite location, when the issue started and any warning displayed. Add a clear photo only when it is safe to take one. Keep people away from unsafe equipment and leave inspection or repairs to qualified personnel.

Note: These are general informational tips, not a substitute for the operator manual, manufacturer instructions or inspection by a qualified technician.
