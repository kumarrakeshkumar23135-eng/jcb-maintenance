Heavy Equipment Repair & Servicing

We provide scheduled preventive maintenance and emergency breakdown repair for backhoe loaders and construction machinery. Select a service category below to understand what is covered during an inspection.

1. Hydraulic System Repair

हाइड्रोलिक सिस्टम रिपेयर: Boom, dipper, and bucket cylinder seal kit replacement, main hydraulic pump pressure testing, control valve servicing, and high-pressure hose fabrication.

2. Scheduled Periodic Service

नियमित सर्विसिंग (250 / 500 / 1000 Hrs): Complete engine oil, transmission fluid, hydraulic filter, fuel water-separator replacement, and multi-point chassis lubrication.

3. Engine & Cooling Diagnostics

इंजन और कूलिंग सिस्टम: Fuel injector calibration, turbocharger inspection, radiator flushing, water pump replacement, and overheating troubleshooting under heavy load.

4. Transmission & Axle Overhaul

ट्रांसमिशन और एक्सल: Torque converter stall testing, shuttle reverser repair, differential gear inspection, wheel hub bearing replacement, and brake disc servicing.

5. Pins, Bushes & Structural Care

पिन और बुश रिप्लेसमेंट: Kingpost, loader arm, and excavator bucket pin/bush replacement to eliminate play and restore digging accuracy.

6. Electrical & Starter Systems

इलेक्ट्रिकल और वायरिंग: Alternator charging checks, starter motor repair, solenoid valve testing, dashboard sensor diagnostics, and work-light wiring.
