About JCB Maintenance

JCB Maintenance is a dedicated heavy equipment repair, diagnostics, and preventive servicing platform built for backhoe loader owners, fleet operators, and construction contractors. We understand that every hour a machine sits idle on a job site directly impacts project timelines and profitability.

हमारा उद्देश्य (Our Mission): तेज़, भरोसेमंद और पारदर्शी ऑन-साइट JCB रिपेयर और मेंटेनेंस सेवा प्रदान करना ताकि आपकी मशीन बिना रुके काम करती रहे।

Who We Are & Our Field Experience

With hands-on field experience servicing 3DX, 4DX, and heavy earthmoving machinery, our technical team specializes in diagnosing complex hydraulic pressure drops, engine overheating, transmission wear, and electrical faults directly at your work site.

Alongside our on-site booking service, we publish practical maintenance checklists and troubleshooting resources—supported by our educational video demonstrations—to help operators catch minor wear before it turns into costly component failure.

Why Operators Trust Us

  • On-Site Field Support: Rapid dispatch to construction sites, quarries, and rural road projects.
  • Transparent Diagnostics: Clear explanation of worn seals, filters, pumps, or pins before any replacement begins.
  • Genuine & OEM-Grade Parts Guidance: Recommendations for reliable hydraulic oils, filters, and undercarriage components.
  • Bilingual Support (English & Hindi): Clear communication with both fleet managers and machine operators.
