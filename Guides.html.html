Operator Maintenance Guides & Checklists

Proper daily inspection and timely fluid changes can double the lifespan of your backhoe loader's hydraulic pump and engine. Use these field guides before starting your shift.

Guide 1: Daily 10-Minute Pre-Start Walkaround (दैनिक जांच सूची)

Before turning the ignition key each morning, operators should complete these six checks:

  1. Engine Oil & Coolant Levels: Check dipstick on level ground and verify coolant expansion tank sits between MIN and MAX marks.
  2. Hydraulic Oil Sight Glass: Park with loader arms down and excavator bucket curled in; verify clear oil visibility in the tank gauge.
  3. Grease All Pivot Points: Apply lithium-complex grease every 10 operating hours to loader arms, kingpost, stabilizer legs, and bucket pins.
  4. Fuel Water-Separator Bowl: Drain any accumulated water or sediment from the bottom valve to protect fuel injectors.
  5. Hydraulic Hoses & Rams: Inspect cylinder rods for nicks and hoses for chafing or weeping fittings.
  6. Tire Pressure & Wheel Nuts: Check front and rear tire inflation and ensure wheel lug nuts are tight.

Guide 2: Standard Service Interval Chart

Operating Hours Key Maintenance Tasks
Every 10 Hours (Daily) Grease all pins/bushes, check engine oil, coolant, hydraulic level, and air filter dust valve.
Every 250 Hours Inspect fan belt tension, battery terminals, transmission oil level, and clean radiator fins.
Every 500 Hours Replace engine oil, engine oil filter, primary/secondary fuel filters, and outer air filter element.
Every 1000 Hours Replace hydraulic return filter, transmission oil & strainer, axle oils, and check valve clearances.

Guide 3: Preventing Hydraulic Overheating in Summer

High ambient temperatures during continuous excavation can thin hydraulic oil and damage pump seals. Always blow out dust from the hydraulic oil cooler core using low-pressure compressed air every 2–3 days, avoid holding control levers at relief pressure against end-stops, and use the manufacturer-recommended ISO VG 46 or VG 68 anti-wear hydraulic oil.
