Frequently Asked Questions (अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न)

Find quick answers regarding our on-site repair bookings, maintenance intervals, and spare parts support.

1. Do you provide on-site repair directly at construction sites?

Yes. Most hydraulic hose replacements, cylinder resealing, filter servicing, and electrical repairs are performed directly at your job site so you do not have to transport the machine to a workshop.

2. How often should I change the engine and hydraulic oil on a JCB 3DX?

Engine oil and filters should be changed every 500 operating hours (or every 250–300 hours in heavy dust environments). Hydraulic return filters are typically replaced at 1,000 hours, while complete hydraulic oil replacement is recommended every 2,000 hours.

3. Why is my backhoe loader losing hydraulic digging power when warm?

Loss of power after the oil warms up usually indicates internal bypass in the main hydraulic pump, worn cylinder piston seals, or a clogged suction strainer. A pressure gauge test isolates the exact component before replacing parts.

4. Can I supply my own spare parts and lubricants for a booked service?

Yes. You can either provide your own genuine JCB or OEM-approved filters and oils, or ask our technician to bring the required seal kits and lubricants based on your machine model.

5. How quickly can a technician reach my site after booking?

For emergency breakdowns, we prioritize same-day dispatch depending on distance. Routine 500-hour and 1,000-hour preventive maintenance visits are scheduled within 24 hours of your booking confirmation.
